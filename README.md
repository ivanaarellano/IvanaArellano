About Me

I'm a computational and translational scientist with nearly nine years of experience in pharmaceutical R&D, specializing in scalable analytical workflows for preclinical research and biomarker discovery.

My background includes Python and R for high-dimensional biological data analysis, including single-cell transcriptomics, bulk RNA sequencing, and quantitative imaging. My research also spans immunology-focused preclinical studies, with experience in neuroinflammation and collaborative biomarker development alongside academic partners and translational research teams.
